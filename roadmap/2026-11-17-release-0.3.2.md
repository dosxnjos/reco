Notas da 0.3.2 (exatas):

```markdown
Echo cancellation no longer makes some recordings worse.

- **Echo cancellation, frame by frame:** when cancelling the PC's sound would leave a stretch louder than what the microphone heard, that stretch keeps the microphone audio instead (#21). On the hardest of 13 real recordings, echo went from 7.8 dB worse to 0.3 dB better, and none of the 13 got worse.

**Install:** download `Reco-Windows-setup.exe` below and run it (per user, no admin). The installer is not signed: if SmartScreen says "Windows protected your PC", click "More info" and then "Run anyway". Recordings and settings are kept.

---

**Português:** o cancelamento de eco deixa de piorar algumas gravações (#21): no trecho em que tirar o som do PC deixaria o áudio mais alto do que o microfone ouviu, fica o áudio do microfone. Baixe o `Reco-Windows-setup.exe` abaixo; se o SmartScreen avisar, clique em "Mais informações" e "Executar assim mesmo".

**Polski:** usuwanie echa nie pogarsza już niektórych nagrań (#21): tam, gdzie usunięcie dźwięku z komputera dałoby głośniejszy fragment niż to, co usłyszał mikrofon, zostaje dźwięk z mikrofonu. Pobierz `Reco-Windows-setup.exe` poniżej; jeśli SmartScreen ostrzeże, kliknij „Więcej informacji”, a potem „Uruchom mimo to”.
```
