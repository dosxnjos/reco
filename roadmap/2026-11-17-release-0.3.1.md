Notas do release (exatas):

```markdown
Two fixes on top of 0.3.0.

- **Recordings no longer die on Stop when the PC is busy.** When the encoder was still writing as Stop closed the file, the MP3 encoder could crash and the recording was lost. Writing and closing now wait for each other (#17).
- **Cleaner transcripts:** stray lines in a script that is not the language's (or only punctuation) are filtered out, in Portuguese, English and Polish (#34).

**Install:** download `Reco-Windows-setup.exe` below and run it (per user, no admin). The installer is not signed: if SmartScreen says "Windows protected your PC", click "More info" and then "Run anyway". Recordings and settings are kept.

---

**Português:** duas correções. A gravação não se perde mais ao Parar com o PC ocupado (#17), e a transcrição deixa de trazer linhas em outro alfabeto ou só com pontuação (#34). Baixe o `Reco-Windows-setup.exe` abaixo; se o SmartScreen avisar, clique em "Mais informações" e "Executar assim mesmo".

**Polski:** dwie poprawki. Nagranie nie ginie już przy zatrzymaniu, gdy komputer jest obciążony (#17), a w transkrypcji nie pojawiają się już linie w obcym alfabecie ani same znaki interpunkcyjne (#34). Pobierz `Reco-Windows-setup.exe` poniżej; jeśli SmartScreen ostrzeże, kliknij „Więcej informacji”, a potem „Uruchom mimo to”.
```
